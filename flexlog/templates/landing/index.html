{# Fake Google.com homepage. Submitting any term either logs the admin in (when the SHA-512 of the term matches the configured admin hash) or 303-redirects to the real Google search results for that term. The page intentionally renders nothing flexlog-specific — title is "Google", the logo is the Google wordmark, the footer matches google.com. The form posts to OUR server (landing.submit), not to google.com — that matters: we need to compute the password hash server-side. Wrong passwords get the Google search redirect from the route handler. #} Google
